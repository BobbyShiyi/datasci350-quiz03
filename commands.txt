On my run, the original script did not raise an exception. Its finish_reason was "stop" because the commentator produced a short valid JSON response that fit within the 15-token limit.

# Changed max_tokens from 15 to 100

python ollama/ask_json.py

New output:
{"answer": "I'm sorry, I do not have enough information to answer your question about git commit."}
stop
I'm sorry, I do not have enough information to answer your question about git commit.

Explanation: A max_tokens limit of 15 can truncate a longer JSON response before it is complete. Increasing it to 100 gives the model enough space to finish the JSON response.
